---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Autonomous Strategy Evolution System turns validated evidence and research into governed improvement proposals.

v3 integrates Trade Physics evolution as current-scope, including:

- deterministic TPS parameter/model proposals;
- directional-speed/time-model experiment proposals;
- learned-model/calibration proposals;
- model suspension/rollback recommendations;
- controlled staging and review.

The word autonomous means autonomous analysis/proposal workflow, not unrestricted production rewriting.

---

## 2. CANONICAL POSITION

`Evidence -> Analytics -> Research -> Strategy Intelligence -> Autonomous Evolution -> Human Approval -> Staging/Rollout/Rollback`

The evolution system is downstream of truth and is not part of the runtime-critical signal-production path.

---

## 3. CORE RULE

The system may:

- observe;
- analyze;
- detect;
- hypothesize;
- simulate;
- design experiments;
- stage approved experiments;
- evaluate;
- recommend;
- recommend rollback/suspension.

It may not silently change production strategy, Trade Physics formulas, model authority or parameters without governed authorization.

---

## 4. INPUT DOMAINS

Evolution may consume:

- Decision Audit;
- Trade Physics/TPS evidence;
- telemetry/outcomes;
- Performance Analytics;
- Research findings;
- model validation/calibration/drift;
- operational/UX evidence where relevant;
- current parameter/canonical versions;
- rollback readiness.

Low-quality or contradictory evidence must reduce readiness rather than be averaged away.

---

## 5. TRADE PHYSICS EVOLUTION CATEGORIES

Potential proposal categories include:

### Deterministic model

- TPS component weights;
- S/T/P caps;
- speed reference;
- directional-speed lookback/recency profile;
- volatility transform;
- future TPS lifecycle policy;
- classical-score/TPS combination policy.

### Structural/time behavior

- available-space policy refinements;
- directional-speed/time adjustments;
- corridor/time interactions.

### Learned intelligence

- model family/features;
- retraining;
- calibration method;
- probability influence proposal;
- drift suspension;
- model replacement/rollback.

Each category is subject to its owning canonical document.

---

## 6. EVOLUTION LIFECYCLE

Canonical lifecycle:

`OBSERVE -> DETECT -> HYPOTHESIZE -> ASSESS READINESS -> DESIGN EXPERIMENT -> APPROVE -> STAGE -> RUN -> EVALUATE -> RECOMMEND -> APPROVE/REJECT -> ROLLOUT/ROLLBACK -> REVIEW`

Every Trade Physics change must be traceable through this lifecycle unless it is a pure defect correction explicitly authorized by governance.

---

## 7. READINESS GATES

Before a Trade Physics mutation may move beyond recommendation, evaluate:

- evidence quality;
- sample adequacy;
- leakage status;
- calibration status where relevant;
- feature/schema consistency;
- current strategy/model drift;
- instrumentation completeness;
- discrepancy contamination;
- side-effect/blast-radius estimate;
- rollback readiness;
- approval chain.

Possible readiness states include:

- NOT_READY;
- RESEARCH_ONLY;
- READY_FOR_LOW_RISK_EXPERIMENT;
- READY_FOR_STAGED_EXPERIMENT;
- READY_FOR_GOVERNED_ROLLOUT_REVIEW.

---

## 8. DETERMINISTIC TPS PARAMETER PROPOSALS

The current proposed defaults are structural constants until Parameter Control canon says otherwise.

Evolution may recommend changes only with:

- evidence basis;
- affected formula/version;
- proposed value;
- expected effect;
- sensitivity/replay result;
- sample/window;
- risks;
- rollback plan.

A recommendation to change a deterministic formula may require a MAJOR canonical successor, not merely a config edit.

---

## 9. DIRECTIONAL SPEED EVOLUTION

The initial proposed directional speed uses:

- 20 M1 intervals;
- linear recency weights 1..20;
- directional positive movement;
- gross-speed comparison.

Evolution may test alternatives, but production change requires evidence that the alternative improves timing without unacceptable side effects.

Any change to the formula must remain synchronized with Time Model and Trade Physics canon.

---

## 10. LEARNED MODEL EVOLUTION

The system may autonomously detect:

- model degradation;
- calibration drift;
- feature drift;
- retraining readiness;
- model-comparison opportunities.

It may trigger/prepare research or training workflows within allowed environments.

It may not promote a new model to live influence without validation and approval.

---

## 11. MODEL READINESS AND AUTHORITY

Model states such as:

- UNTRAINED;
- INSUFFICIENT_DATA;
- TRAINED_UNVALIDATED;
- VALIDATED_RECOMMEND_ONLY;
- APPROVED_FOR_BOUNDED_USE;
- SUSPENDED_DRIFT

must be respected by evolution logic.

A lower-readiness model cannot be automatically escalated because a new training run completed.

---

## 12. MODEL REPLACEMENT

A candidate replacement model must have:

- immutable artifact/version;
- dataset/feature lineage;
- target definition;
- validation/calibration evidence;
- baseline comparison;
- drift analysis;
- approval state;
- rollback target.

Replacement is a governed rollout, not file overwrite.

---

## 13. BOUNDED USE

The Intake source allows a conceptual bounded-auto-adjust mode.

v3 interprets this strictly.

Bounded use is permitted only when active canon explicitly defines:

- eligible parameter/action;
- lower/upper bounds;
- evidence/readiness threshold;
- maximum change rate/frequency;
- automatic stop triggers;
- rollback behavior;
- event/audit requirements;
- human override.

Until such bounds exist, bounded auto-adjust is not authorized.

---

## 14. OWNER / ADMIN AUTHORITY

Owner remains final authority for material production strategy evolution.

Principal/authorized admins may perform only actions granted by role and parameter/change-control canon.

The evolution engine must expose who approved what and under which version/bounds.

---

## 15. EXPERIMENT BRANCHING

Trade Physics experiments should use explicit experimental/staging identity separated from production.

Each branch/experiment requires:

- branch/experiment id;
- parent strategy/canonical version;
- exact diff;
- hypothesis;
- success/failure criteria;
- rollback target;
- final disposition.

---

## 16. REPLAY / SIMULATION

Candidate changes should be evaluated using replay/simulation when possible.

For Trade Physics, replay must measure:

- score/TPS differences;
- time-state differences;
- rejection/promotion changes;
- signal volume;
- objective outcomes;
- segment/regime effects;
- disagreement patterns;
- learned model impacts if applicable.

Simulation is evidence, not proof of production safety by itself.

---

## 17. RECOMMENDATION BUNDLE

A governance-ready Trade Physics recommendation must include:

- recommendation id;
- problem/finding;
- evidence summary;
- confidence;
- current vs proposed behavior;
- affected canon/parameters/code;
- model/feature version where relevant;
- expected benefit;
- side effects;
- validation plan;
- rollback plan;
- approval requirement.

---

## 18. ROLLBACK

Every material Trade Physics rollout must have a known rollback target.

Rollback may include:

- restore previous deterministic parameter bundle;
- restore previous strategy version;
- disable learned-model influence;
- restore previous model artifact;
- suspend model to recommend-only;
- revert directional-speed policy if validated rollback criteria trigger.

Telemetry/evidence should continue where safe so rollback does not destroy learning.

---

## 19. POST-ROLLOUT REVIEW

After any governed change, monitor:

- signal volume;
- rejection stage distribution;
- TPS distribution;
- directional-time changes;
- outcome performance;
- calibration/drift;
- discrepancy/missingness;
- operational impact.

The system should be able to recommend rollback when failure triggers are met.

---

## 20. SAFETY MECHANISMS

Required:

- approval gates;
- staged deployment;
- version checkpoints;
- rollback instructions;
- post-change monitoring;
- drift watch;
- immutable audit trail;
- fail-closed model authority;
- owner override.

---

## 21. FORBIDDEN BEHAVIORS

The evolution system must never:

- silently rewrite live TPS weights;
- silently change directional-speed formula;
- promote learned probability to decision authority without approval;
- hide failed experiments;
- erase old model artifacts/provenance;
- mutate production because a single window improved;
- bypass Parameter Control bounds;
- rewrite historical decision/outcome truth;
- treat AI recommendation as approval.

---

## 22. OBSERVABILITY

Material evolution actions must be auditable:

- finding detected;
- hypothesis/recommendation created;
- readiness evaluated;
- experiment approved/staged;
- model trained/validated;
- change approved/rejected;
- rollout started/completed;
- monitoring alert;
- rollback/suspension.

Exact event contracts belong to consolidated Event Schema.

---

## 23. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS

At minimum:

1. recommendation cannot directly mutate production;
2. readiness gates block insufficient evidence;
3. Trade Physics formula changes identify canonical impact;
4. experimental branch is separated from production;
5. model promotion requires artifact/provenance/validation;
6. bounded use is impossible without explicit bounds;
7. rollback target is known before rollout;
8. failed experiments remain recorded;
9. drift can suspend model authority;
10. Owner/Admin permissions are enforced and auditable.

---

## 24. FINAL PRINCIPLE

The system may continuously improve Trade Physics, but improvement follows:

`EVIDENCE -> RESEARCH -> PROPOSAL -> APPROVAL -> STAGING -> VALIDATION -> ROLLOUT -> MONITORING -> ROLLBACK/KEEP`.

Autonomy accelerates disciplined work; it never replaces governance.
